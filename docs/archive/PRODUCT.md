# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local service business owners (dental practices, trades, personal services) who need websites that generate trust, bookings, and operational clarity. They are practical operators, not designers. They evaluate on credibility, clarity, and whether the thing actually works — not on aesthetics for aesthetics' sake.

Secondary users: existing Anvis clients accessing the Client Portal to track project stage, payments, and updates.

## Product Purpose

Anvis builds and improves websites for local service businesses. The public site is the primary sales and trust surface — it needs to move visitors toward a free audit or checkout. The Client Portal is a lightweight project hub for active clients.

Success on the public site = a visitor understands the offer, trusts the operator, and takes a next step (audit or checkout).

## Brand Personality

Sharp. Calm. Operational.

Voice: concise, direct, no hype, no generic agency language, no over-explaining. Speaks like a strategic operator, not a creative agency.

Emotional goal: confidence and clarity. The visitor should feel like they found someone who understands their business problem and has a system to solve it.

## Anti-references

- Generic Webflow agency template look
- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetic on client-facing pages
- Startup gradient overload
- Crypto/web3 dark aesthetic
- Overly futuristic AI agency look
- Flashy animation-first portfolio style
- Squarespace-style generic service business site
- Bloated corporate consulting site
- Luxury brand minimalism that sacrifices clarity
- Design that looks like it's trying to impress other designers

## Design Principles

1. **Conversion before decoration.** Every section exists to move the visitor toward a decision. Visual elements support that; they never compete with it.
2. **Hierarchy through restraint.** Amber accent is used on key phrases and offer names — not paragraphs, not decorations. Sparing use makes it land.
3. **Operational credibility.** The site should feel like a system, not a portfolio. Structure, clarity, and completeness signal that Anvis actually knows how local service businesses work.
4. **Premium without vague.** Dark, clean, well-spaced — but always specific. Vague copy signals a generalist. Specific copy signals expertise.
5. **Sequential clarity.** The path from landing to checkout should be obvious at every step. No dead ends. No ambiguous CTAs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Reduced-motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable. Color is never the only signal (amber accents paired with text weight).
