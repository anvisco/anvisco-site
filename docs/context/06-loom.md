# Loom

## Duration

- 60 to 90 seconds.

## Style

- Specific.
- Helpful.
- No overproduction.
- No filler.

## Flow

1. Show the issue.
2. Explain the booking, trust, or visibility impact.
3. Add one positive note.
4. End with a clear CTA to the free snapshot, call, or site.

## Guidance

- Keep the recording tight and practical.
- Use the video to make the problem obvious, not to perform.
