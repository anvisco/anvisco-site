# Audit Framework

## What to check

- Booking flow.
- CTA clarity.
- Mobile experience.
- Trust signals.
- Service structure.
- Local discovery.
- Google, AI, and Maps readiness.
- Speed and performance.
- Content hierarchy.
- FAQ and schema readiness where relevant.

## Output by offer

- Cold outreach: 1 to 3 issues.
- Free Snapshot: 3 to 5 findings.
- Paid audit: a deeper ranked action plan.

## Audit lens

- Focus on what is blocking visibility, trust, or bookings.
- Keep the findings specific and actionable.
- Prefer the highest-leverage fix over a long list of minor notes.
