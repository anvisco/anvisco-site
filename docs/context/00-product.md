# Product

## Brand

- Public brand name: Anvis.
- Use `Anvis` in public-facing copy and internal guidance.
- Keep `anvisco.com` and `brian@anvisco.com` as the domain and email references.

## Core line

- Anvis builds websites that run, grow, and optimize local businesses.

## Positioning

- Run: support the real operating flow, booking flow, and client or patient journey.
- Grow: improve trust, conversion, clarity, and inquiries.
- Optimize: keep improving structure, performance, and updates over time.
- Get Discovered: help businesses show up in Google, AI tools, Maps, and local recommendations.

## Target market

- Local businesses.
- Priority focus: dental and local service businesses.
- Expansion targets: physio, chiro, med spas, contractors, wellness, and other service operators.

## What Anvis is not

- Not a generic web design agency.
- Not AI SEO hype.
- Not guaranteed rankings.
- Not "traditional SEO is dead" messaging.
- Not overpromising.
- Not generic agency language.

## Voice

- Direct.
- Premium.
- Operational.
- Practical.
- Concise.
- No hype.

## Decision rule

- If a message does not help the business run, grow, optimize, or get discovered, remove it.
